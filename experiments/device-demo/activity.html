“Working” is a hook-based inference, not a CPU or terminal activity check. Work expires after 10 minutes without another event; input alerts after 30 minutes. A session can remain listed after it becomes inactive. Rejected events are older than events already processed. Events before this diagnostic was installed are unavailable.
